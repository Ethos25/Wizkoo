The Story

Three Copywriting Approaches

Draft 1: The Absurdity
He was almost four. He was reading. At his private school assessment, they put him in a room with seventeen computers, and then rejected him because he wanted to touch them.

He could do the work. He just couldn't sit still.
- And they said the children they accept do.
The research had started two years prior. But that was the day we knew something had to break.
Wizkoo is what four years of it built.
A PreK-5 mastery framework wrapped in a week that moves the way kids actually move.
