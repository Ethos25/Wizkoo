FAQ Concepts — The Dark Phase

Concept 1: The Split-Pane (Copenhagen Restraint)

The details.

No hiding behind clicks. Just the answers.

What ages does this work for?

Families with children ages 3 to 12. Activities matched to each child's age, reading level, and stage.

What makes this a system and not just a curriculum?

A curriculum tells you what to teach. This builds the actual week around your children: their ages, reading levels, and the theme you chose. Week 8 references Week 3. Every subject connects through a single thread. Nothing is taught in isolation.

How is AI used?

AI personalizes. Educators designed the methodology. You review everything before your children see it.

How do I know my child is actually learning?

Every week you'll receive an Orbit Report showing exactly what your child learned. Not screen time logged. Actual facts, vocabulary, and discoveries.

Questions parents ask.

What ages does this work for?

Is Wizkoo secular or faith-based?

Wizkoo is secular. Science-based. Fact-based. Built for curious families.

Do I need to be a teacher?

No. Every activity includes what to do, how long, and what done looks like.

Can I use this for weekends or after school?

Yes. Homeschool, after school, summer. Same engine, your schedule.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

A prompt gives you a lesson. Wizkoo gives you a system: multi-age differentiation, spiral learning across weeks, Atlas and Elementum built in, and plans that remember last month.

Is my children's data safe?

We keep two things about your child: a first name and an age. That is the entire file. No last name, no birthdate, no photos. Encrypted and yours to delete any time.
